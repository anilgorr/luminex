# Questions for Luminex

Answers to 1–3, 6–7, 10–11 and 16 unblock the first five location pages — ask for those first.

## Pricing and products

1. What's the price range per sq ft for uPVC sliding, casement and tilt & turn windows?
2. What's the range for aluminium sliding, casement and thermal-break windows?
3. Is Bhutan priced differently from India? Do you quote in rupees or Ngultrum?
4. Which profile, glass and hardware brands do you use in each system? Do you have tested U-values or acoustic ratings?
5. Which glass options do you offer: double glazing, low-E, toughened, acoustic?

## Delivery and coverage

6. What's the typical time from order to installation for Siliguri, Guwahati, Shillong, Gangtok, Darjeeling, Jalpaiguri, Thimphu, Paro and Phuentsholing?
7. Do you have a staffed office, dealer or own install team in Guwahati, Thimphu, Paro or Phuentsholing? Or is everything served from Siliguri?
8. Is there a real Bhutan office address behind the +975 number?
9. For Bhutan orders, what's the route, what paperwork is needed, what taxes or fees apply, and how long does it take door to door?

## Proof

10. Can you share 3–6 completed projects per city, each with photos, locality, product and year? Client names are optional.
11. Do you have any hotel, resort or commercial projects in Bhutan? We need at least two for the hotel page.
12. Can we get 3–5 real customer testimonials, with written permission to publish?
13. Do you have your own photos of the doors shown on the website? The current door photos and text may come from another company's site.

## Warranty and service

14. What are the full warranty terms: what's covered, what isn't, and does it start from manufacture or installation?
15. How do service calls work in Bhutan: who comes, and how quickly?

## Website details

16. Which phone numbers and emails are correct? The site shows two sets: +91-1263598995 / +91-51253265610 and +91 960 9988 749 / +91 993 3581 666, plus both allyunitygroup.com and allunitygroup.com.
17. Where should website enquiries go: which email, WhatsApp number or CRM?
18. The Windows, Renovations, Gallery and Luminex Difference pages don't exist on the current site. We've drafted them — can you review and approve the content?
19. Do you want a team section? If so, send names, roles and photos.
20. Can you share the Vercel account the site should go live on, and who manages the domain?
